## 🔬 Concepts (Mechanisms & Computational Methods)

- [[enzymatic-inactivation]]: Degradation or chemical modification of antimicrobial molecules (e.g., beta-lactamase hydrolysis, transferases).
- [[target-modification]]: Structural alteration, mutation, or replacement of cellular drug targets to eliminate binding affinity.
- [[efflux-pumps]]: Active transmembrane extrusion systems reducing intracellular antibiotic concentrations.
- [[bypass-mechanisms]]: Acquisition or expression of alternative, drug-insensitive metabolic enzymes circumventing chemical blocks.
- [[pairwise-alignment-screening]]: Bioinformatics local alignment (Smith-Waterman) and dual-threshold candidate screening (identity & coverage).
- [[genomic-qc-metrics]]: Sequence quality metrics including nucleotide distributions, GC content percentage, and ambiguous base detection.
